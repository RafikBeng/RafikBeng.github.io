Who I am



I'm a software developer and network engineer based in Algiers, Algeria. My work sits at the intersection of mobile development, Linux systems, and low-level networking.

I tend to go deep — reverse-engineering router firmware to understand its auth protocol, compiling Asterisk from source for ARM, or porting custom recovery to a TV box. If something doesn't have a ready-made solution, I build one.

Outside of code, I'm interested in VoIP infrastructure, home server setups, and Android device modification.
